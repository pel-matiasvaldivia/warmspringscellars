The wine club, and the books

You have arrived here from QuickBooks. This is the Warm Springs Cellar wine club service — the software the winery uses to run its own club. It is not sold, listed or offered to anyone else: it serves one winery, and the QuickBooks connection exists so that the club's invoices and payments reach that winery's own books without being typed in twice.

What the connection does

When a release ships and a member's card is charged, the club raises an invoice and records the payment that settles it. Both are pushed to QuickBooks Online: the invoice against the member as a customer, and the payment linked to that invoice.

  • It writes two kinds of document — invoices and payments — and nothing else.
  • It reads only enough to find the customer and the items it must post against. It never changes anything it did not create.
  • It will not create items. Which income account wine revenue lands in is the bookkeeper's decision, so the items must already exist in QuickBooks or the push stops and says so.
  • Nothing is lost if the connection is down. Documents wait in a queue the winery can see and export, and go across when it is restored.

For the winery

The connection is managed from the cellar desk, which is staff-only and asks for a password. It is where the company is authorised, where the queue is shown, and where a document can be pushed or retried by hand.
