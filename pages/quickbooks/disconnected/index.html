Disconnect

The club no longer reaches QuickBooks

You disconnected the wine club from QuickBooks Online. Intuit has revoked the club's access to the company, and it takes effect immediately.

What this did not do

  • Nothing was deleted. Every invoice and payment already in QuickBooks stays in QuickBooks. They are the company's documents, not the club's.
  • The club kept running. Members are still members, releases still ship, cards are still charged. The accounting connection is not on that path and never was.
  • Nothing is being lost now. Invoices and payments raised from here on collect in the club's own queue, which exports as a spreadsheet. They can be pushed across whenever the connection is made again.

One thing left to do

This page is shown by Intuit after a disconnection, and anyone can arrive at it with a web address — which is why it does not touch the club's records. Acting on an unauthenticated page like this one is how a connection gets broken by a stray link rather than by a decision.

So the club still holds its now-dead copy of the credentials. Clear it from the cellar desk: open the books screen and press Disconnect there too. The queue is kept.

Open the books screen

To connect again later — the same company or a different one — use Connect to QuickBooks on that same screen. Reconnecting does not re-send anything that already went across: a document QuickBooks already holds is adopted rather than written a second time.
