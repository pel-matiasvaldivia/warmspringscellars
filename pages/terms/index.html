These terms govern membership of the Warm Springs Cellar wine club and the use of this website. They are between you and Warm Springs Cellar, 1310 Warm Springs Rd, Glen Ellen, CA 95442. Joining the club means accepting them.

You must be 21

Wine is sold only to people aged 21 or over. By applying you confirm that you are 21 or over and that the person who will receive the shipment is too. Every delivery requires the signature of an adult aged 21 or over — the carrier will not leave wine at a door, and a package no one signs for comes back to us.

Applying is not joining

Anyone may apply. The winery decides who is offered an allocation: the club is small, the wine is finite, and we may decline or place an application on the waiting list without giving a reason. An offer we make is held open for a limited period stated in the invitation, and lapses if it is not taken up.

Where we can ship

We ship only within the United States, and only to states where the winery is licensed to ship wine. The address you give is checked against that list when you apply, and an address we cannot ship to is declined there rather than after you have paid. We cannot ship to a PO box, and we cannot ship outside the United States.

If you move, tell us before the next release. An allocation sent to an address you have left is not one we can recover.

What a membership is

Three shipments a year — spring, fall and Christmas — of the allocation you chose. What goes in each box is the winery's choice from the current release, and for some allocations includes wines that are never sold publicly. We do not promise a particular wine, a particular vintage or a particular bottle count beyond the size of the allocation.

Allocation sizes and their prices are shown on the club page at the time you join, and on the offer we send you. They can change for future releases; a change is told to members before the release it applies to, and a member who does not want it can leave before being charged for it.

How payment works

This is not a subscription. Nothing is charged when you join. You save a card, and it is charged when a release actually ships — because until the wine exists there is nothing to charge for. There is no recurring billing date and no standing authority beyond the three releases a year described above.

  • Your card is entered on a page hosted by Stripe and stored by Stripe. The winery never sees or holds the number.
  • Each release is charged once, at the price shown for your allocation, plus carriage to your address less whatever credit towards carriage your allocation carries. An invoice is emailed with the receipt.
  • If the card is declined we write to you and try again once the card is fixed. Nothing ships until it is paid.
  • If your bank asks for you personally — a verification step — we will send you a link rather than retry in the night, because retrying cannot work.

Pausing and leaving

Write to wineclub@warmspringscellar.com or telephone 707.318-0167. There is no notice period, no fee and no minimum number of shipments. A membership ended before a release is charged owes nothing for it. A membership ended after a release has been charged is ended for the releases after it; the one already paid for still ships.

The winery may also end a membership — if we cannot ship to you, if a card cannot be charged over more than one release, or if the club changes shape. You will not be charged after that.

If something is wrong with the wine

Tell us within 30 days of delivery. Wine that arrives broken, that arrives heat-damaged, or that is faulty in the bottle is replaced or refunded — our choice, and we would usually rather send the wine. Keep the bottle and the packaging; a photograph helps and is usually all we ask for.

Because of the law on alcohol we cannot accept a return of wine you simply did not care for, and we cannot accept a return of wine that has left your possession. A shipment refused at the door, or returned because nobody of 21 could sign for it over the carrier's attempts, can be reshipped at your cost or refunded less carriage.

Tastings and events

Complimentary tastings and member events are a courtesy of membership, not a purchased good. They are subject to availability and to an appointment; the winery is open by appointment only. They have no cash value, do not accumulate, and end with the membership.
