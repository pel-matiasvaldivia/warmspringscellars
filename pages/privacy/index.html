Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 November 2026.

Warm Springs Cellar is a family winery in Glen Ellen, California. This page describes what we collect from the people who write to us and who join the wine club, what we do with it, and who else sees it. It is written to be read rather than to be survived, and it describes what the software we run actually does.

Who is responsible. Warm Springs Cellar, 1310 Warm Springs Rd, Glen Ellen, CA 95442. Questions about anything here, including a request to see or delete your information, go to wineclub@warmspringscellar.com or 707.318-0167.

What we collect

When you ask about the club Your name, email address, telephone number if you give one, and the state you live in. The state is asked because we may only ship wine to certain states.
When you join The shipping address for your allocation, and which allocation you chose.
When you pay The record that a payment was made, for how much, and whether it succeeded. We never see or hold your card number. The card is entered on a page hosted by Stripe and stored by Stripe; the club holds only an identifier that lets Stripe charge it when a release ships.
When we send you email Whether the message was delivered, whether it bounced, and whether you unsubscribed. We keep unsubscribes and hard bounces permanently, because that is the only way to be sure we do not write to you again.

What we do not collect

This website sets no cookies. There is no analytics script, no advertising pixel and no third-party tracker on any page of it. We do not build a profile of you, we do not track you across other websites, and we have nothing to ask your consent for, which is why no banner asks you for any.

Why we hold it

  • To answer you, and to run the club you joined: pack the right wine, ship it to the right address, and charge the card you left for it.
  • To keep the books. A winery that sells wine has to keep records of its sales, and those records name the buyer.
  • To obey the law on shipping alcohol, which requires us to know the state we are shipping to and to insist on an adult signature on delivery.
  • To write to you about the club, if you asked us to or if you are a member — and to stop, permanently, the moment you say so.

Who else sees it

Only the companies that do a specific job for us, and only the part they need for it. We do not sell your information, we do not rent it, and we do not share it for anyone else's advertising.

Stripe Takes and stores your card, and charges it when a release ships. Stripe receives your name and email address.
Intuit (QuickBooks Online) The winery's accounting system. It receives your name, email address, city and state, and the amount of each invoice and payment.
Our email provider Delivers the club's mail, and tells us what bounced or was unsubscribed. It receives your name and email address.
The shipping carrier Receives your name and delivery address, and records the adult signature the law requires.

We will also hand over records when the law requires it — a tax authority, the alcohol regulator, or a court order. That is rare and we would rather tell you it can happen than leave it out.

How long we keep it

Records of sales — invoices, payments and shipments — are kept for as long as tax and alcohol-beverage law requires us to keep them, which is years rather than months. We cannot delete those on request and no winery can.

Everything else is yours to end. An enquiry that never became a membership, the contact details of a member who has left, a shipping address that is no longer current: ask and we will remove them. What stays behind is the sales record the law requires, and the unsubscribe entry that keeps us from writing to you again — keeping that one is respecting your choice.

Your rights

If you live in California, the California Consumer Privacy Act gives you the right to know what personal information we hold about you and where it came from, to have it corrected, to have it deleted within the limits above, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We do not sell or share personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of — but the right exists and we will answer as though you had exercised it.

We will not treat you worse for asking. There is no different price and no lesser service for a member who has asked a question about their own information.

To exercise any of this, write to wineclub@warmspringscellar.com. We may ask you to confirm the request from the address we hold, because handing someone's records to whoever asks for them would be its own violation.

Email from us

Every message we send carries an unsubscribe link and our postal address. Unsubscribing takes effect for everything that is not about an order you have already placed: we will still tell you that your allocation shipped and still send the receipt for it, because that is part of the sale and not marketing.

You must be 21

This is a winery. Nothing here is directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 21. If you believe a minor has given us their details, write to us and we will remove them.

Security, honestly stated

The club's records sit on an encrypted connection, behind a password, on a server the winery controls, and they are backed up. Card numbers are not there to be stolen because we never hold them. No one can promise that a system cannot be broken into, and we are not going to be the first; what we can say is which of your information would be exposed if it happened, and the list is the one above.

Changes

If this policy changes we will post the new version here with a new date at the top. A change that materially affects members will be sent to members by email rather than left for them to find.
