Releases

Compose a release, raise its orders, tell the members, then charge them. Four presses, not one: the composition says what each tier's shipment holds, raising it takes those bottles out of the cellar and raises orders only for the members there is wine for, and nothing is charged until somebody has looked at what was raised. Running any of them twice is safe.

Tell them is the one the club has promised in writing since the beginning — the welcome letter, every receipt and the offer page all say we write two weeks before a release so a member can change quantities or skip the season. Press it after raising the orders and give them those two weeks. It quotes each member their own wine, their own carriage and their own total, and asks them to reply; replies reach the club mailbox. It writes only to members who have not been charged yet, so pressing it twice cannot warn somebody about money that has already gone.
