Thank you for writing to us.
Warm Springs Cellar is a small winery in Glen Ellen. We make about a thousand cases a year and nearly all of it goes directly to the people on our allocation list. There is no shop and no mailing list to join — there is the list, and a short form that asks to be on it.
Request an allocation
Or paste this into your browser: warmspringscellar.com/#join
It takes a minute. Every request is read by hand, and you will hear back about what is open and what it costs — we do not hold a waiting list you never hear from. One thing worth knowing first: we can only ship where direct wine shipping is permitted, and the form will tell you straight away if your state is not one of them.
If you were writing about something else — a visit, an order already placed, a question about a wine — this is the only automatic message you will get from us. Your note is in front of a person, and you will have a proper answer from them.
