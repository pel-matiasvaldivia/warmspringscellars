Dear {{first_name}},
Cecilia Valdivia has opened her own winery, and she wanted the people she knows to hear it from her rather than come across it somewhere.
Warm Springs Cellar is a return to the tiny winery in Glen Ellen where PJ and Robert began their winemaking journey over 40 years ago. Cecilia and Robert Rex have been collaborating on winemaking for nearly two decades. As Robert prepares for retirement from Deerfield Ranch Winery, Cecilia is stepping into the next chapter of her winemaking career; until his departure he is serving as consulting winemaker for Warm Springs Cellar, bringing decades of experience to this new venture.
The first bottling features seven varietals, each from a vineyard we have cultivated a long relationship with, and all of them in Sonoma County:

And Coffee
An unexpected pairing, perhaps, but wine lovers tend to appreciate good coffee. Robert’s winemaking began in the 1970s in a Berkeley garage, in the neighbourhood where Alfred Peet was roasting; Mr. Peet was a friend and mentor, and Robert has been devoted to the craft ever since. He has his own blend, the Rex Blend, and Cecilia has hers — Ceci’s Blend.
Wine club members receive samples of both with their allocation shipments, whole bean. We are still adding the coffee to the website, so stay tuned.
