WHO THIS IS FOR, and why it is a second letter rather than an edit of the
first one. new-kid-on-the-block.* is written to the Deerfield list, and its
whole frame rests on one fact — Cecilia posted a printed announcement to
every one of those people, and the email is the follow-up to something
already in their hands. These 102 are Cecilia's own contacts. NOBODY ON THIS
LIST HAS HAD A LETTER. So the sentence that makes the first letter work is
not merely unnecessary here, it is untrue, and three more lines that assume a
shared history go with it:

  * "that distinctive Sonoma character you've come to appreciate" -> they
    have not been drinking these wines.
  * "Thank you for your continued support." -> there is none to continue.
  * "Your details are already in the form" -> no personalised link is built
    for this wave, so the form is empty.

What replaces the opening says why they are hearing from the winery, in one
sentence, because that is the question a reader asks first and the one a
platform's review asks too. CECILIA MUST CONFIRM THAT SENTENCE IS TRUE OF
EVERY NAME ON THE LIST before this goes out. If some of these people are
hers and others came from somewhere else, they are two mailings, not one.

AN INVITATION FROM GLEN ELLEN

Dear {{first_name}},

Cecilia Valdivia has opened her own winery, and she wanted the people she
knows to hear it from her rather than come across it somewhere.

Warm Springs Cellar is a return to the tiny winery in Glen Ellen where PJ
and Robert began their winemaking journey over 40 years ago. Cecilia and
Robert Rex have been collaborating on winemaking for nearly two decades. As
Robert prepares for retirement from Deerfield Ranch Winery, Cecilia is
stepping into the next chapter of her winemaking career; until his departure
he is serving as consulting winemaker for Warm Springs Cellar, bringing
decades of experience to this new venture.

    Peterson Vineyard, Sonoma Valley

    Deerfield Vineyard, Sonoma Valley

  2024 Zinfandel
    Los Chamizal Vineyard, Sonoma Valley

  2023 Pinot Noir
    Four Palms Vineyard, Russian River Valley

  2023 Malbec
    Running Rabbit Ranch, Sonoma Valley

  2023 Sangiovese
    Alpicella Vineyard, Fountain Grove District, Sonoma Valley

  2021 Cabernet Sauvignon
    Wolf Run Vineyard, Sonoma Valley

The inaugural release is limited to 850 cases, and these wines go to wine
