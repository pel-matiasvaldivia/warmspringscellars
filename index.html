Our Philosophy

Small by Design,
Not by Accident

Since 1982, Warm Springs Cellars has chosen restraint over scale. We farm select vineyard blocks across Sonoma County, hand-picking fruit at dawn and crafting each wine with the patience it demands. One thousand cases is not a limitation. It is a commitment.

Single-Vineyard Focus

Each wine tells the story of one vineyard, one season. Wolf Run in Sonoma Valley for our Cabernet Sauvignon. ElieO in Russian River Valley for our Pinot Noir.

Winemaker Direct

No middlemen, no retail shelves. Every bottle travels from our cellar to your table. Our members know the hands that made their wine.

Four Decades of Craft

Forty years of learning Sonoma's soils, microclimates, and rhythms have taught us when to act and when to wait. That patience lives in every glass.

The Wines

Two wines. Two vineyards. Each vintage a singular expression of place and season.

2019

Cabernet Sauvignon

Wolf Run Vineyard · Sonoma Valley

Dark cassis and dried violets open to a palate of black cherry, espresso, and graphite. Twenty-two months in French oak gave this wine its architecture; the volcanic soils of Wolf Run gave it its soul.

2021

Pinot Noir

ElieO Vineyard · Russian River Valley

Ripe Bing cherry and wild strawberry meet hints of forest floor and baking spice. Whole-cluster fermentation adds a savory spine. Delicate yet persistent, this is Russian River at its most transparent.

520 cases produced

Questions

Frequently Asked

How does the allocation work?

You'll receive two shipments per year, in spring and fall. Each shipment is curated based on your chosen tier. We notify you in advance with the option to customize quantities before we charge and ship.

Where do you ship?

We ship to all states where direct-to-consumer wine shipping is permitted. Currently, we ship to 44 states. Contact us to confirm availability in your state.

Is there a commitment?

We ask for a minimum commitment of two shipments (one year). After that, you may cancel anytime with 30 days' notice before the next scheduled shipment.

Can I visit the winery?

Members enjoy private, by-appointment visits at the estate. We welcome you and up to two guests (four for Founder's Reserve members). Please book at least one week in advance.

Can I give a membership as a gift?

Yes. Gift allocations are available for both tiers. We'll include a personalized card with the first shipment. Contact us for details.

How is wine shipped?

All wines ship via temperature-controlled carriers. During summer months, we use insulated packaging and may hold shipments if temperatures are extreme. An adult signature is required.
