The Winemakers

Two Paths, One Cellar

A chemist from Iowa and a winemaker from Argentina. Bound by a shared conviction: that great wine is made in the vineyard, finished with patience, and shared with the few who truly appreciate it.

Photo: Robert Rex

Robert Rex

Founder & Winemaker

A native of Iowa who found his calling in Berkeley, Robert discovered wine through a tasting club while studying Chemistry at UC Berkeley in 1961. By 1976, he was a partner in Berkeley's first commercial winery, where he trained under the legendary André Tchelistcheff — the father of modern California winemaking.

A true Renaissance man — chemist, graphic designer, woodworker, and accomplished gourmet cook — Robert brings a scientist's precision and an artist's intuition to every vintage. His five decades of winemaking experience culminate in Warm Springs Cellars, where small lots and single vineyards allow him to practice the kind of uncompromising craft that larger operations cannot sustain.

Photo: Cecilia Valdivia

Cecilia Valdivia

Co-Founder & Winemaker

Born in Argentina and raised in the wine traditions of South America, Cecilia brings a worldview to Sonoma County winemaking that bridges continents. Her palate was shaped by the Malbecs of Mendoza before she crossed into California's coastal valleys, where she found the cool-climate elegance that matched her vision.

At Warm Springs Cellars, Cecilia is the hands in the vineyard and the instinct in the cellar. Her approach is tactile and intuitive — she walks the rows, tastes the fruit daily during harvest, and makes blending decisions that honor each vineyard's voice. Together with Robert, she has built a winery where science and sensibility meet in every bottle.

“We don't make wine for everyone. We make it for the few who understand that the best things in life are worth waiting for.”
Robert Rex & Cecilia Valdivia

Our Philosophy

Small by Design,
Not by Accident

Since 1982, Warm Springs Cellars has chosen restraint over scale. Robert and Cecilia farm five vineyard blocks across Sonoma County, hand-picking fruit at dawn and crafting each wine with the patience it demands. One thousand cases is not a limitation. It is a commitment.

Single-Vineyard Focus

Each wine tells the story of one vineyard, one season. Wolf Run and Los Chamizal in Sonoma Valley, Four Palms in Russian River Valley, Alpicella and Running Rabbit Ranch in Fountain Grove.

Clean Winemaking

Drawing from Robert's chemistry background and Cecilia's instinct for balance, we craft wines that are low in sulfites and histamines — pure expressions of fruit and terroir, nothing more.

Winemaker Direct

No middlemen, no retail shelves. Every bottle travels from our cellar to your table. Our members know the hands that made their wine.

The Wines

Five wines. Five vineyards. Each bottling is a single season from a single site, and when it is gone it is gone.

2021

Cabernet Sauvignon

Wolf Run Vineyard
Sonoma Valley

A mountain-grown blend from one of the region’s premier ridge-line vineyards, built by taste rather than formula: 81% Cabernet Sauvignon with Merlot, Cabernet Franc and Syrah. Sonoma Valley is more than the valley floor, and this wine is the ridges talking.

13.3% alc. 1,440 bottles
2023

Malbec

Running Rabbit Ranch
Fountain Grove, Sonoma County

A west-facing slope in the new Fountain Grove AVA, carved out of Sonoma Valley for its own terroir. Whole-berry fermentation and gentle cold pressing keep the dark colour and the blackberry Malbec is known for — Cecilia’s grape, grown here.

14.1% alc. 588 bottles
2023

Pinot Noir

Four Palms Vineyard
Russian River Valley

The Pacific’s marine layer blankets this valley most summer mornings, and the cool microclimate shows: delicate, intensely aromatic, wonderfully complex. It pairs with a wider range of food than almost any other wine. Don’t be afraid to serve it slightly chilled.

16.1% alc. 1,380 bottles
2023

Sangiovese

Alpicella Vineyard
Fountain Grove, Sonoma County

A steep slope near the top of Hood Mountain in the Mayacamas, facing the ocean and the setting sun, rooted in sparse volcanic soil. Ripe strawberry that expands across the palate like few other wines. Pair it with anything; share it with friends.

13.6% alc. 1,750 bottles
2024

Zinfandel

Los Chamizal Vineyard
Block 7 · Sonoma Valley

A tribute to Peter Haywood, who planted Los Chamizal in the neighbourhood where Sonoma Zinfandel began in the 1870s and worked with Robert for four decades. Block 7 was Robert’s favourite of the eight. It’s bold, it’s complex, and the flavour goes on forever.

16.1% alc. 1,650 bottles

Questions

Frequently Asked

You'll receive two shipments per year, in spring and fall. Each shipment is curated by Robert and Cecilia based on your chosen tier. We notify you in advance with the option to customize quantities.

We ship to all states where direct-to-consumer wine shipping is permitted. Currently, we ship to 44 states. Contact us to confirm availability in your state.

We ask for a minimum commitment of two shipments (one year). After that, you may cancel anytime with 30 days' notice before the next scheduled shipment.

Members enjoy private, by-appointment visits at the estate and our cellar cave. We welcome you and up to two guests (four for Founder's Reserve members).

Robert's chemistry background informs our approach: minimal intervention, low sulfites, no additives. Our wines are crafted to be pure expressions of fruit and place — nothing artificial, nothing hidden.

All wines ship via temperature-controlled carriers. During summer months, we use insulated packaging and may hold shipments if temperatures are extreme. An adult signature is required.
